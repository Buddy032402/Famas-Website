About the club

We tell stories through craft, camera, and collaboration.

The Filmmakers' Society of Assumption College of Davao brings together students who are passionate about storytelling, creativity, filmmaking, and visual expression. Through collaboration, mentorship, and experimentation, the club creates space for ideas to become moving stories.

From concept to final cut, members explore the craft of visual media while encouraging growth, creativity, and community among student creators.

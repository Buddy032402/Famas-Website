How to join

Current membership process.

The club's current recruitment steps are confirmed by the moderators and should be checked before participating in activities. Students who are interested in joining should express their interest through the official Messenger inquiry channel and follow the current instructions provided by the club.

  1. Share your interests and background through the official Messenger inquiry channel.
  2. Wait for the club to confirm the current application or interview process.
  3. Follow the official next steps provided by the club moderators.

FAQs

Common questions before you apply.

Do I need prior experience to join?

No prior experience is required to express interest. Students are welcome to explore roles and learn collaboratively.

Can I join if I am interested in a specific role?

Yes. Students may share their areas of interest, such as cinematography, production, editing, or storytelling, through the official inquiry channel.

How will I know the next steps?

The club will confirm the current membership process and any next steps through its official communication channels once the request is reviewed.

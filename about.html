ABOUT THE CLUB

BEHIND EVERY STORY IS A VISION.

Introduction

We create space for student storytellers to explore the language of film.

The Filmmakers' Society of Assumption College of Davao serves as a creative space for students who are interested in understanding how stories are shaped through film. The club supports students in exploring storytelling, cinematography, directing, editing, sound, and production as meaningful forms of artistic expression and communication.

Through collaboration, experimentation, and creative practice, the club helps students deepen their interest in filmmaking and visual media while creating opportunities to learn, share ideas, and develop their own voice as creators.

Mission

Proposed purpose for review.

The club aims to nurture students' interest in filmmaking and storytelling by providing a welcoming environment for learning, creating, and collaborating. It supports the development of technical skills, visual literacy, and creative confidence through student-driven projects, screening opportunities, and peer engagement.

Vision

Proposed direction for review.

Creative growth

To inspire students to become thoughtful creators who use film as a way to reflect, imagine, and communicate ideas with clarity and purpose.

Responsible storytelling

To encourage storytelling that is intentional, respectful, and grounded in awareness, empathy, and responsible media practice.

Student culture

To cultivate a student-centered creative culture where collaboration, curiosity, and expression help strengthen artistic confidence and community.

Core values

Values that guide our work.

C

Creativity

We encourage creative exploration and the courage to experiment with visual ideas and storytelling form.

T

Teamwork

Strong filmmaking grows through shared effort, idea-sharing, and mutual support among student creators.

R

Responsibility

We value mindful practice, honest work, and thoughtful decision-making in every stage of creation.

R

Respect

We foster a culture of listening, constructive feedback, and respect for people, ideas, and differences.

S

Meaningful storytelling

We believe stories should be reflective, engaging, and sensitive to the lived experiences they represent.

F

Faith and solidarity

In a spirit of community and shared purpose, students are encouraged to support one another with humility, care, and collective encouragement.

Filmmaking process

From idea to exhibition.

01

Concept

Ideas begin with observation, inspiration, and the search for a story worth telling.

02

Pre-production

Planning includes script development, visual direction, scheduling, resource planning, and team roles.

03

Production

Filming brings the story to life through cinematography, sound, performance, and directorial guidance.

04

Post-production

Editing, sound design, color, and polishing shape the final visual and emotional experience of the work.

05

Exhibition

Final projects are shared through screenings, presentations, celebrations, and community viewing.
